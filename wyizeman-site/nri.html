Invest in India without managing India from abroad.

Real-estate investment becomes considerably harder when the investor is thousands of kilometres away. Wyizeman acts as a local investment and transaction coordination point for NRIs investing in Indian real estate.

Before you invest

We help investors examine opportunities before capital is committed.

  • Opportunity Identification
  • Investment Evaluation
  • Developer Assessment
  • Location & Market Analysis
  • Commercial Comparison
  • Remote Consultation

During the transaction

Distance should not mean lack of visibility. Depending upon the transaction, we assist or coordinate around:

  • Documentation
  • Transaction Process
  • Power of Attorney Requirements
  • Registration Coordination
  • FEMA-Related Requirements
  • Taxation & Accounting
  • Professional Legal Support

After the investment

Our role can continue after the transaction.

  • Possession Coordination
  • Leasing Assistance
  • Tenant Coordination
  • Property-Related Support
  • Resale
  • Exit Assistance

Specialist matters. Specialist professionals.

Where legal, taxation, accounting or FEMA advice is required, Wyizeman coordinates with appropriately qualified professionals rather than attempting to replace them.
