Where we focus.

We do not attempt to cover every segment of real estate. Our focus remains on areas where we believe disciplined selection can create meaningful opportunities for private investors.

Premium Plotted Developments

Land can be a compelling long-term asset. But buying land inside a branded development does not automatically make it a compelling investment. We look for opportunities where multiple factors align.

What we evaluate

  • Developer & Execution Capability
  • Location & Connectivity
  • RERA & Applicable Approvals
  • Current and Future Infrastructure
  • Entry Price
  • Comparable Supply
  • End-User & Investor Demand
  • Development Timeline
  • Likely Holding Period
  • Resale & Exit Potential

Income-Producing Commercial Real Estate

A leased property is only as attractive as the quality and sustainability of the income behind it. Headline yield is therefore only the beginning of our evaluation.

What we evaluate

  • Tenant Quality
  • Lease Tenure
  • Lock-In Period
  • Rental Escalation
  • Current Yield
  • Building & Asset Quality
  • Micro-Market
  • Current and Replacement Rentals
  • Operating Costs
  • Vacancy Risk
  • Resale Demand
  • Exit Potential
