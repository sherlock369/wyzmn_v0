Real estate before it becomes inventory.

CORE by Wyizeman works at the intersection of land, development and private capital to originate select real-estate opportunities.

Where opportunities begin

Most investors encounter real estate after a project has been conceived, structured and brought to market. CORE operates further upstream. We identify situations where land, development capability and capital can be brought together to create a viable real-estate opportunity.

Three components

Land

Identify and evaluate strategically located land and aggregation opportunities.

Development

Work with selected developers with the capability and commitment required to acquire, develop and monetise the opportunity.

Capital

Where appropriate, enable select investors to participate through project-specific structures.

Selective by nature. Relationship-led by design.

CORE is not an open marketplace. Opportunities are presented selectively based on the nature of the project, investment structure, availability and suitability of the investor. Participation is primarily relationship-led.

For Developers

CORE also works with selected developers seeking access to suitable land and strategically structured development opportunities. Our role may include:

  • Land Identification
  • Land Aggregation
  • Commercial Structuring
  • Private Capital Coordination
  • Project Strategy
